Acme Robotics — Employee Handbook Excerpt

Paid Time Off
Full-time employees accrue 1.5 days of paid time off (PTO) per month, up to
a maximum of 18 days per year. PTO must be requested at least 5 business
days in advance through the HR portal, except in cases of illness. Unused
PTO up to 5 days may be carried over into the next calendar year; anything
beyond that is forfeited on December 31st.

Remote Work Policy
Employees may work remotely up to 3 days per week without prior approval.
Fully remote arrangements require manager sign-off and are reviewed every 6
months. Remote employees are expected to be reachable during core hours,
10 AM to 4 PM in their local time zone, and to attend all-hands meetings
with video on.

Expense Reimbursement
Business expenses under $75 can be submitted with a receipt through the
expense portal and are typically reimbursed within 5 business days.
Expenses over $75 require pre-approval from a manager. Personal software
subscriptions, commuting costs, and meals during normal working hours are
not reimbursable.

Sick Leave
Employees receive 10 paid sick days per year, separate from PTO. Sick leave
does not need to be requested in advance and does not require a doctor's
note for absences of 2 days or fewer. Unused sick leave does not carry over
or pay out at the end of the year.

Parental Leave
Employees who have completed at least 6 months of service are eligible for
12 weeks of paid parental leave, which can be taken within the first 12
months after the birth or adoption of a child.
