Acme Robotics — New Hire Onboarding Guide

Before Day One
IT will ship a laptop and any required hardware to arrive at least 2 days
before your start date. You will receive an email with temporary login
credentials for the company email and HR portal 24 hours before you start.

Day One
Report to the front desk at 9:30 AM for badge photo and building access
setup. Your manager will walk you through your first-week schedule, which
includes an HR orientation session, a benefits enrollment walkthrough, and
introductions to your immediate team.

Account Setup
New hires must complete account setup for: company email, Slack, the HR
portal, and the internal engineering wiki. Access to specific engineering
systems (source control, cloud infrastructure, internal APIs) is requested
by your manager and typically granted within 2 business days of your start
date.

Equipment
Standard equipment includes a laptop, a monitor, and a headset, all shipped
to your home address or available for pickup at the office. Additional
equipment requests (standing desk, second monitor) go through the facilities
request form and are approved by your manager.

Benefits Enrollment
Health, dental, and vision benefits must be elected within 30 days of your
start date or you will need to wait for the next open enrollment period.
401(k) enrollment has no deadline and can be set up at any time through the
HR portal.

First 30 Days
By the end of your first 30 days you should have completed all onboarding
training modules, met with your manager for a first check-in, and set your
first quarter goals together.
