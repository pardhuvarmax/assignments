Nimbus Cloud Storage — Frequently Asked Questions

What is Nimbus?
Nimbus is a cloud file storage and sync service. Files placed in the Nimbus
folder on your computer are automatically uploaded and kept in sync across
all devices signed in to the same account.

What plans are available?
Nimbus offers three plans: Free (5 GB storage, single device), Plus (200 GB
storage, up to 5 devices, $4.99/month), and Pro (2 TB storage, unlimited
devices, version history for 180 days, $14.99/month). All paid plans include
a 14-day free trial and can be cancelled at any time from the billing page.

What is the refund policy?
Annual subscriptions can be refunded in full within 30 days of purchase.
Monthly subscriptions are not refundable but can be cancelled at any time,
and access continues until the end of the current billing period.

How much can I upload at once?
The web uploader accepts files up to 10 GB each on Plus and Pro plans, and
2 GB each on the Free plan. The desktop sync client has no per-file limit
other than available storage quota.

What happens if I delete a file?
Deleted files move to the Trash and are kept for 30 days before permanent
deletion. Pro plan users can restore any previous version of a file for up
to 180 days using version history.

How do I contact support?
Support is available via chat and email, Monday to Friday, 9 AM to 6 PM
Eastern Time. Pro plan customers get priority queueing and a same-day
response target. Free plan customers should expect a response within 3
business days.

Is my data encrypted?
Yes. Files are encrypted in transit using TLS and at rest using AES-256.
Nimbus does not have access to the encryption keys used for the optional
"Locked Folder" feature available on Pro plans.
